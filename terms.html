Bølge — Terms of Use

Last updated: April 2026

Bølge provides access to publicly available internet radio streams.

All streaming content is provided by third-party radio stations. The developer does not control, host, proxy, or rebroadcast any audio streams and does not guarantee stream availability or accuracy.

Content and Third-Party Streams

Bølge provides access to publicly available internet radio streams published by their respective broadcasters. The app does not host, store, reproduce, redistribute, or retransmit any audio content.

Audio playback is performed exclusively using the system media player (AVPlayer) directly from the original stream URLs provided by broadcasters or their authorized infrastructure.

The app does not establish a second connection to the radio stream for playback or transcription in the App Store version.

Live transcription uses system speech recognition. Audio used for transcription is derived from the same playback pipeline. The app does not record or store that audio, and does not send stream audio to servers operated by Bølge or the developer for transcription. Processing through Apple’s speech services, where applicable, is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy and your device settings.

If audio capture required for transcription is unavailable, the app will continue playback without transcription.

All content rights remain with their respective owners. The app acts solely as a client to access publicly available streams.

The developer is not responsible for interruptions, stream availability changes, or content transmitted by third-party radio stations.

If you are a rights holder or represent a radio station and would like your stream or logo removed from the application, please contact the developer. Verified requests will be processed promptly and the content will be removed.
